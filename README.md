# Diwali Sales Analysis

## Introduction

This project involves **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a Diwali sales information dataset. The goal of the analysis is to identify significant sales trends and patterns that can help businesses optimize their sales strategies during the Diwali festival.

### Objectives:
- Understand customer demographics that influence sales.
- Analyze product sales patterns.
- Identify key sales-driving factors and popular products.
- Draw actionable insights to improve marketing strategies.

## Steps Involved in the Analysis

### 1. Data Cleaning and Preprocessing
- Loaded the dataset and performed necessary cleaning operations:
  - Handled missing values.
  - Converted data types where required.
  - Removed unnecessary columns.
  
### 2. Data Exploration and Visualizations
- **Demographic Analysis**:
  - Analyzed customer demographic data (age, gender, marital status, etc.).
  - Created visualizations to understand the distribution of customers by age group, gender, and occupation.
  
- **Product Sales Analysis**:
  - Identified top-selling products and product categories.
  - Visualized total purchases and sales for various product categories.

### 3. Key Insights
- **Top Spending Customers**:
  - Visualized customers who contributed the most to sales.
  
- **Sales by Age Group**:
  - Customers in the **age group 26-35 years** were found to contribute the highest sales.
  
- **Sales by Gender**:
  - Sales were higher among **male customers** compared to female customers.
  
- **Sales by State**:
  - **Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka** recorded the highest sales.

- **Popular Products**:
  - Identified the top 10 most sold products.

## Conclusion

- **Target Audience**: Married women in the age group **26-35 years** from **Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka**, working in **IT, Healthcare, and Aviation**, were identified as the key demographic driving sales. 
- **Popular Categories**: The **Food, Clothing, and Electronics** categories are the most purchased items during Diwali.
- **Sales Strategy**: Businesses can focus their marketing efforts on these key demographics and product categories to maximize sales during the Diwali festival.
